# OnPitch — Product Brief (summary)

Competitive 6-a-side recruitment, not casual pickup. Existing apps (Footy Addicts, Find a Player) have thin/stale Canterbury–Thanet coverage and no skill filtering.

**MVP:** self-rated profiles · tiering · call-and-response matching · app-owned roster with WhatsApp handoff after confirmation · fixture-change notifications.

**Monetisation (undecided):** ~£1 pay-per-slot, or free basic tier with paywall/sponsorship for higher tiers.

**Go-to-market:** Phase 0 manual recruitment in Thanet → QR plaque at games (name + number) → Phase 1 Facebook ads → explore Leisure Leagues/Powerleague as a channel.

**Roadmap:** Stage 1 player recruitment only. Stage 2 pitch/league operation (trigger: Jackie Baker 3G resurface).

**Out of scope V1:** pitch booking, venue management, payments beyond slot fees, formal venue/league relationships.
